# Plan amendment 002.1: TRV-like simulated thermostat

Status: APPROVED 2026-10-21
Amends: .sdd/plans/002-e2e-testing-PLAN.md (approved 2026-10-20)
Implements: spec amendment .sdd/specs/002.2-trv-like-thermostat.md

## What changes
1. **Section 2, Simulated devices table** becomes:

| Device | Kind | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `input_boolean.e2e_house_occupied` | `input_boolean/create` | house occupancy |
| `input_boolean.e2e_hot_water` | `input_boolean/create` | hot water entity |
| `input_number.e2e_target_temp` | `input_number/create`, 5 to 35, step 0.5 | holds the simulated thermostat's target |
| `climate.e2e_thermostat` | template helper (config flow `template`, menu option `climate`). Modes heat, auto and off. Its target temperature reads `input_number.e2e_target_temp`, and its set-temperature action writes it. No heater | the heating room's climate entity, standing in for a TRV |

   If the template climate helper needs somewhere to hold its mode, `input_select.e2e_thermostat_mode` (options heat, auto, off) is created for it. The template flow on the test instance lists a `climate` menu option (seen in the 2026-10-21 runs); its form fields are not yet known. If this HA's template climate flow cannot provide modes heat, auto and off with a settable target, the implementer stops and asks the user. There is no silent fallback.

   Removed from the table: `input_boolean.e2e_heater`, `input_number.e2e_room_temp`, `sensor.e2e_room_temperature`, and the generic thermostat.
2. **Section 2, Rooms**: `e2e-heating` gets climate `climate.e2e_thermostat` only, with no temperature sensor.
3. **Section 2, fresh-start**: "sets `climate.e2e_thermostat` to HVAC mode `heat`" stays. The reason becomes "so the room is in heating mode, as a TRV set to heat would be".
4. **Section 2, Baseline state**: unchanged, apart from the thermostat now being the template one.
5. **Section 3, Decisions**: D8 is added. "Simulated thermostat: generic thermostat with a heater switch, or a template climate with no heater. Choice: template climate (user, 2026-10-21). It mirrors a TRV; the integration only sets the target. Scenario 23 is dropped (spec amendment 002.2)."
6. **Section 4, thermostat.spec.ts**: scenario 22 only. A block covering now is created on `e2e_heating`, and the test waits up to 75 seconds for `climate.e2e_thermostat`'s target to equal the block temperature. The scenario 23 bullet is removed.
7. **Section 4, installer.spec.ts**: the "not offered" list becomes `input_number.e2e_target_temp`, `climate.e2e_thermostat`, and `input_select.e2e_thermostat_mode` if created.
8. **Sections 1, 2 and 5**: "23 scenarios" becomes "22 scenarios"; "Scenarios 1 to 23" becomes "Scenarios 1 to 22"; in section 2 Benchmarks, "1" to "23" becomes "1" to "22".
9. **Section 6, Risks**:
   - "Helper config flows differ by HA version" now names the template climate flow in place of the generic thermostat.
   - In the midnight risk, the scenario list "(11, 16, 19, 20, 22, 23)" becomes "(11, 16, 19, 20, 22)", and "22 and 23" becomes "22".
   - The cleanup of leftovers also removes `e2e_` entities from earlier runs that are no longer created (`input_boolean.e2e_heater`, `input_number.e2e_room_temp`, the `e2e_room_temperature` template sensor), because cleanup matches the `e2e_` prefix. No other helper is touched.

## Why
User decision 2026-10-21, spec amendment 002.2.

## Supersedes
The plan 002 sections and rows named above. Everything else is unchanged.
