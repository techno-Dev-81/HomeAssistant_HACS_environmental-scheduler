# Spec amendment 002.2: TRV-like simulated thermostat, scenario 23 dropped

Status: APPROVED 2026-10-21
Amends: .sdd/specs/002-e2e-testing.md (approved 2026-10-20), after amendment 002.1

## What changes
1. **Scenario 23 is dropped.** It checked that the thermostat calls for heat when the room is below target. That is the thermostat's own behaviour (in production, the TRV's), not the integration's. The suite has 22 scenarios: the 21 from tasks amendment 001.1, and scenario 22.
2. **R6a** becomes: each run creates, or recreates if left from an earlier run, simulated devices from HA helpers:
   - `input_boolean.e2e_house_occupied` (house occupancy);
   - `input_boolean.e2e_hot_water` (hot water);
   - a simulated TRV-like thermostat `climate.e2e_thermostat`, built with HA's template helper, with modes heat, auto and off, a target temperature the integration can set, and **no heater**, plus only the helper entities it needs to hold its own state.

   No physical device is used. No heater switch is created.
3. **R8** keeps scenario 22 ("when a block starts in a heating room, the room's thermostat target becomes the block temperature") and removes scenario 23. Its introduction "for these two new scenarios" becomes "for this new scenario".
4. **R10a**: "including scenarios 22 and 23" becomes "including scenario 22".
5. **Section 7**: the list of helpers the suite creates becomes `input_boolean.e2e_house_occupied`, `input_boolean.e2e_hot_water`, `input_number.e2e_target_temp`, `climate.e2e_thermostat`, and `input_select.e2e_thermostat_mode` if the template climate needs it.
6. **Section 12, E2E**: "the 23 end-user scenarios" becomes "the 22 end-user scenarios".
7. **R6b**: "the 23 scenarios need none" becomes "the 22 scenarios need none".
8. **Section 9, Out of scope** adds: controlling the house heat source (for example a heat pump) from room demand, GitHub issue #29.

## Why
User decision 2026-10-21. HA's generic thermostat helper accepts only a switch or fan as its heater, so scenario 23 would have needed a test-only heater switch. In production the user's TRVs heat by themselves, and the integration only sets their target. The user wants the suite to test what production needs. Controlling the house heat source from room demand is a separate feature, logged as GitHub issue #29.

## Supersedes
In spec 002: R6a; R6b's scenario count; scenario 23 and the "two new scenarios" wording in R8; the "scenarios 22 and 23" wording in R10a; the helper list in section 7; the scenario count in section 12; section 9 gains one line. Everything else, including amendment 002.1, is unchanged.
